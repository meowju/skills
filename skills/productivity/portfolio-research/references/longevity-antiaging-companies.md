| 2024-25 | SToMP-AD | D+Q | 5-20 | CNS penetrance shown, efficacy unclear | Nature Medicine |

**Key insight:** The only published "biological age reversed in humans" data is the 2019 TRIIM trial, n=9, never replicated at scale. Everything else is biomarker changes, mouse data, or preclinical.

## Red-flag phrases to watch for in news

- "First human trial of [pill that] reverses aging" → almost always fabricated or grossly overstating a Phase 1 safety trial
- "Pill restores hair and muscle" → never been an RCT endpoint in any senolytic/metformin/Rapamycin trial
- "Cures biological age" → meaningless; biological age is a measure, not a disease
- "FDA approved [longevity drug]" → FDA does not approve "longevity" as an indication; it approves disease treatments
- "Doctors stunned" / "scientists baffled" → content farm tell; ignore

## Investment thesis skeleton (if user asks)

- **Pure-play exposure is sparse.** Most "longevity" public names (UBX, CRSP, NTLA) are clinical-stage biotechs with binary risk. None have approved anti-aging products.
- **Pick-and-shovel is safer.** NVDA (BioNeMo + drug discovery compute), TSM (foundry), Illumina (sequencing) all benefit from longevity R&D without binary clinical risk.
- **ETF baskets for diversification.** ARKG and IDNA are the broadest pure-play options.
- **The 2026-2027 catalyst calendar is dense.** Multiple first-in-human readouts in the next 12-24 months. Use the `portfolio-research-cron` setup to track tickers and the `stock-fundamental-due-diligence` skill for deep dives.

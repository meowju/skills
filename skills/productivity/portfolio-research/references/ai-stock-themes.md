## BUY Signal Criteria for AI Stocks
A ticker qualifies as BUY when ALL of:
- RSI(14) < 50 (not overbought) AND RSI trending up
- Price within 15% of 200-day SMA (not far below = no deep downtrend)
- MACD bullish or just flipped positive
- Walk-forward OOS Sharpe > 0.5
- Strong sector-confirmed tailwind

⭐ STRONG BUY = all criteria met + confirmed by walk-forward OOS validation + regime filter pass

## Alerts to Flag
- RSI > 70 (overbought) or RSI < 30 (oversold) → flag per ticker
- MACD bearish/bullish crossover triggered this run
- Gap > 3% overnight
- FDA decision upcoming / clinical trial results
- Earnings within 7 days
- Unusual volume spike (>2x average)

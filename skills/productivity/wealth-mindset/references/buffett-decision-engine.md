# Buffett's Decision Engine: The 4 Business Tests, 2-List Strategy & Daily Execution

### Buffett's Decision Engine: The 4 Business Tests

Before buying any business, Buffett runs four tests. If any fail, he walks away regardless of price:

1. **Can I understand it?** — Circle of Competence. He skips businesses he can't model in 5 minutes: "If you need a computer to evaluate it, you don't understand it."
2. **Does it have favorable long-term economics?** — Durable moat. What prevents competitors from destroying the economics? The best: switching costs, network effects, brand, regulatory moats.
3. **Is the management competent and honest?** — He only invests with people of integrity. "When searching for the manager, look for someone who is instinctive, rational, and principled."
4. **What's the price?** — He won't overpay even for an excellent business. "Price determines the investment, not the business quality alone."

### The 2-List Strategy: How He Prioritizes

Buffett uses a brutally simple prioritization system:

1. **Write down your top 25 goals.** Everything you want to accomplish.
2. **Circle the top 5.** These get 100% of your focus.
3. **Everything else is a "avoid at all costs" list.** Not "later" — *actively avoid*. The danger is that the #6–25 items distract from the top 5. Buffett's rule: "The difference between successful people and very successful people is that very successful people say no to almost everything."

### Daily Execution: How Buffett Actually Spends His Time

- **80% of his day is reading** — annual reports, newspapers, industry publications. "I read everything. I read all day."
- **No to 99% of deals** — He describes himself as "legally disabled from doing deals" because he won't do anything he doesn't fully understand.
- **Capital allocation is his comparative advantage** — He doesn't manage operations; he allocates capital. Berkshire's structure: insurance subsidiaries generate float, operating businesses generate cash, corporate office allocates capital to highest-return opportunities.
